The problem

Most websites start aging the day they launch.

Pages pile up, patterns drift and visitors stop finding what they came for. And as more customers ask AI assistants instead of searching, a site that isn't AI-ready gets skipped. It still runs — it just stops working for the people, and the tools, looking for you.
